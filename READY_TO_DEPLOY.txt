YOU NEED THESE 5 ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES
================================================================================

1. DATABASE_URL
   - From: https://console.neon.tech
   - Example: postgresql://user:password@host/dbname

2. BETTER_AUTH_SECRET
   - Generate: openssl rand -base64 32
   - 32+ random characters

3. NEON_AUTH_COOKIE_SECRET
   - Generate: openssl rand -base64 32
   - 32+ random characters

4. NEXT_PUBLIC_ETHEREUM_RPC_URL
   - From: https://www.alchemy.com or https://infura.io
   - Example: https://eth-mainnet.alchemyapi.io/v2/YOUR_KEY

5. NEXT_PUBLIC_CONTRACT_ADDRESS
   - From: Deploy smart contract OR use placeholder
   - Example: 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000
